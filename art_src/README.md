# Building models (art_src)

Every placeable building is generated by a Blender Python script from a shared kit of parts. Godot ignores this
folder (`.gdignore`); the finished `.glb` files are copied to `data/models/buildings/<name>/`.

Style rules: `docs/game/art-direction.md` (grey by default, two paint slots, silhouette carries identity).

## Build one

From the repo root, in Git Bash, with Blender 5.2 installed:

    sh art_src/build.sh crafter hero back high scale painted

This runs `art_src/crafter/crafter.py`, writes `SM_Crafter.blend` + `.glb` next to it, prints a validation report
(triangles, n-gons, open edges, bounds, origin), renders review shots to `art_src/crafter/renders/` and copies the
.glb to `data/models/buildings/crafter/`. Render views: hero back left right high close scale painted idle.

**First rule: every part has a mechanical job. See FUNCTION.md (part -> job). No line there, no part.**

## The part audit (enforced)

Every part is declared in its generator with `kit.part(prefix, job, on, joint, joins=())`: what it does, what it is
mounted on, how (ground, seated, bolted, welded, clamped, hinged, pivot, through, inset). `art_src/audit.py` then checks
the real meshes and the build refuses to export while it finds anything:
UNDOCUMENTED (no job), NO MOUNT, FLOATING (> 1.5 mm off its mount), CUTS INTO (parts sinking into each other without a
joint that allows it), NO HARDWARE (bolted joint without bolts, welded joint without a bead), LOOSE BOLT/WELD,
BURIED BOLT, HIDDEN (a part completely inside another). Every build writes `art_src/<name>/parts.md`, the part sheet.

    sh art_src/build.sh smelter --blockout   # stage 1: big parts only, flat colours, part sheet, audit
    sh art_src/build.sh smelter --audit      # generator + audit only, seconds
    sh art_src/build.sh smelter hero close   # full build (refuses on audit problems; AUDIT=warn for WIP)

The skill `~/.claude/skills/blender-game-models` (SKILL.md + LESSONS.md) holds the whole workflow and every lesson.

## Surface and style rules (kit.finish applies the first three to every model)

- Chamfers are doubled (`BEVEL_SCALE`) with 3 segments, so edges read at game distance.
- Wear is baked (Cycles, 2048 px) into a fresh UV0, two textures per model:
  - `T_<model>_D`: final albedo with the default grey paint, paint chipped to bare steel on worn edges, small
    chips on flat panels, grime and AO in the gaps, dust low on the body (world height; `kit.DUST_AMT` is lower
    for ship parts), rust in sheltered dirty spots.
  - `T_<model>_RM`: R = paint mask (1 = intact paint), G = roughness, B = metallic. glTF reads G/B as its
    metallicRoughness texture and ignores R.
- Paint tool (Godot shader, to write when the tool is built): `albedo = D * mix(1, paint / default_grey, RM.r)`.
  Each paint material carries its default grey as the custom property `default_rgb`. `render_building.py`'s
  "painted" view already uses exactly this maths, so chipped edges stay bare steel when painted.
- Emissive and glass are not baked.
- Armour plates (`kit.plate`): proud plates with clipped corners and hex bolts in the corners, optional vent slots.
  `kit.housing` puts them on every face; `skip_faces` leaves room for ports ('-Y0' = front face, lower band).
- No random greebles: no beacons, lugs, side screens, arrows or lone hazard tabs. Every building gets one status
  light (`kit.status_strip`) set into the front trim. Hazard stripes only where something is dangerous.

## Shapes (no stacked boxes)

- `kit.loft(rings)` skins a solid through cross-sections; `kit.squircle(a, b, n)` makes rounded-square sections
  (n=2 circle, ~4 rounded square), `zring`/`yring` place them. Housings swell above the base and taper to the top;
  the Smelter cowl blends the square housing into the round crucible; turret heads are lofted shells with a nose.
- `kit.profile(pts2d, ...)` extrudes any silhouette (the cockpit's raked nose and chin).
- `kit.gusset(...)` adds machined triangular braces (housing corners, press columns, garage towers).
- `kit.pipe_port` / `kit.pipe_collar`: the one pipe-end connector, and it is round everywhere. Every pipe, merger,
  splitter and machine input/output uses the same collar (r 0.30 / 0.232, 0.07 deep, seal ring, 8 bolts) with its
  mating face exactly where the pipe starts, so joints are flush. Junctions (merger, splitter) are round drums; their
  ports grow out of the drum with a saddle weld. Machines use `kit.machine_port`: the neck goes through the housing
  wall at pipe height (0.45 m), welded all round, collar on the footprint edge. A lit collar marks an output.
  No square chutes or docks: a pipe system that is round must connect to round ports.
  `art_src/inspect.py` renders 10 close angles of any model for geometry checks.

## Animations

Moving parts are separate meshes (`kit.assign(group, *name_prefixes)`), with their origin on the joint
(`pivots`), nested where needed (`parents`). `kit.spin()` / `kit.swing()` keyframe them. Each track is exported as
one glTF animation; names ending in `-loop` loop automatically on Godot import. Spinners always fill their track
with whole turns, so every part in a loop stays in sync.

| Model | Animation | What moves |
|---|---|---|
| Smelter | `Working-loop` (1 s) | exhaust fan |
| Crafter | `Working-loop` (4 s) | robot arm sweeps between bed and part, roof fan |
| Constructor | `Working-loop` (2 s) | ram + piston press stroke (0.17 m) |
| Splitter | `Working-loop` (1.5 s) | distributor rotor |
| Ship Core | `Working-loop` (3 s) | containment rings |
| Gyroscope | `Working-loop` (6 s) | outer ring on X, inner ring on Z (nested) |
| Ship Garage | `Working-loop` (4 s) | both crane trolleys travel and dip their tools |
| Gun Turret / Cannon | `Fire` (0.2 s / 0.7 s) | barrels recoil; the `_Barrels`/`_Barrel` mesh is a child of `_Head` |

In game: play `Working-loop` on the building's AnimationPlayer while `Building` is crafting, stop (or pause)
when idle; play `Fire` from the turret's fire code. Turret aiming stays code-driven on the `_Head` node.
Heat/status glow and the thruster flame are not keyframed: pulse the emissive energy from code.
`blender -b -P art_src/base_scene.py -- <repo> --anim` renders the outpost working (`art_src/outpost_working.mp4`).

## Whole-set check

    blender -b -P art_src/base_scene.py -- <repo root as a Windows path>

imports every exported .glb into a small outpost (foundations, machines, pipe line, turrets, ship on the garage)
and renders `art_src/base_overview.png`, `base_ground.png` and `base_ship.png`. `python art_src/contact_sheet.py`
builds `contact_sheet.png` from the per-building renders.

## Pipes in the game

Build mode (first person, aim with the crosshair), pipe selected: click start, click end; the next pipe starts where the last ended. **B** switches
STRAIGHT (level or vertical) and FREE (any angle, slopes). Starting on the end of a pipe at another angle shortens it
and adds a knee bent to exactly that angle (any direction; 2-150 degrees). Supports are placed every <= 2.5 m on
pipes within 30 degrees of level, posts reaching down to whatever is below. Items travel the whole path, knees
included; pipes chain by geometry (relink_all). Test: `godot --path . res://scenes/dev/pipe_test.tscn`.

## Building pieces

Foundation (0.25 m), Foundation 1 m, Foundation 2 m, Frame Foundation 2 m, Ramp 1 m, Stairs 1 m: all 2 x 2 m, snap
edge to edge with their tops lined up, R turns them in quarter turns, nothing mounts on ramps or stairs.

## In the game

`python art_src/wire_scenes.py` wired every scene (re-runnable, skips scenes already wired): it removes the
placeholder mesh and labels, instances the .glb as `Model` (pipes: as `Body` at 0.45 m so span scaling still
applies), resizes the collision box and gives always-running models `scripts/world/model_autoplay.gd`.
Rebuilding a model only needs `build.sh` (it copies the .glb); Godot reimports it on the next editor or headless run.

- Godot strips `-loop` on import, so the animation is called `Working`. Code matches `begins_with("Working")`
  and sets the loop mode itself.
- `building.gd` plays `Working` while a recipe is active and pauses it when idle; `pipe_splitter.gd` while it
  holds an item; `turret.gd` yaws `*_Head`, pitches `*_Cradle` (`_sync_model_head`) and plays `Fire`.
- Checks (Godot 4.7.2 at `C:\Tools\Godot\`):
  `godot --headless --path . --script res://scripts/dev/check_building_models.gd` (every scene loads, has its
  model, animations and turret parts) and `godot --path . res://scenes/dev/model_showcase.tscn` (spawns all of
  them, runs the animations, aims the turrets, saves screenshots to `art_src/ingame/`).
